Current and Recent Projects

AKI4KMU

Project coordinator. Research on the application and transfer of artificial intelligence methods in the context of small and medium-sized enterprises.

Virtual Institute for Networked Mobility

Project coordinator within digitalise_swf. Research on networked mobility and the engineering of intelligent, connected systems.

EKI

Work package lead for energy-efficient artificial intelligence in the BMBF lighthouse project EKI. The work addresses methods for integrating AI into resource-constrained and cyber-physical systems while considering energy efficiency and system requirements.

5G-Landwirtschaft-ML

Former project coordinator. Research at the intersection of 5G communication, machine learning and smart farming, with a focus on intelligent and networked agricultural systems.

Relevant Earlier Research Projects

SPES 2020 and SPES_XT

Project management and contributions to architecture and modeling activities. The projects addressed model-based engineering methods for software-intensive embedded systems and the systematic development of complex technical systems.

ARAMiS

Project management and contributions to architecture and modeling activities in the context of dependable multicore-based systems and embedded-system engineering.

Collaborative Research Centre SFB 614 — Self-Optimizing Systems in Mechanical Engineering

Coordination of the overarching B project “Design Methods and Tools” and leadership of subproject B1 “Design Techniques”. The research developed engineering methods for self-optimizing systems, including model-based specification, software engineering and autonomous adaptation.
